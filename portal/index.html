      Import a spreadsheet

      Save your price list as CSV (in Excel or Numbers: File › Export › CSV), then choose it below. Rows with the same sku as an existing product update it.

      Download a template

      Columns
      type
      The kind of product, from the list in the template (for example floor-lvp or “Luxury vinyl plank”). Required.
      title
      Your product's name, as shoppers should see it. Required.
      price
      Price for one unit, in dollars. Required.
      unit
      What one unit is: box, gallon, piece, roll… Defaults to the usual unit for the type.
      coverage
      What one unit covers: square feet (linear feet for trim, 1 for doors). Defaults to a typical figure; please give your own.
      sku
      Your own product code, so later imports update instead of duplicating.
      size
      For example 24 × 24 in. Optional, but shoppers can filter by it.
      brand, description, image_url, in_stock
      Optional. in_stock is yes or no.

      Keep it in sync automatically

      Keep your price list in a Google Sheet, or anywhere with a link to a CSV file. Roomwise re-reads it every night and updates your prices and stock.

      How to set it up
      1. Use the same columns as the template above. Rows with a type are added or updated.
      2. A plain export from your shop system (Square, Shopify and others) with SKU, price and quantity works too: it updates the price and stock of products you've already added with the same SKU.
      3. In Google Sheets: Share › General access › Anyone with the link (Viewer), then Copy link and paste it below.
